Story 1 · Client service & problem-solving

Dispatch Routing Error Resolved

The situation was that a new employee made a dispatch routing error, causing two boxes of books to be addressed to the same library, one correctly and one by mistake.

My task was to plan and coordinate the rerouting while keeping everyone informed.

I took action that same day by sending a prepaid forwarding label to the receiving library. I rang the next morning to explain how to relabel the parcel and hand it straight back to the postman rather than accept delivery.

The result was that costs, delays and client inconvenience were minimised. A high standard of professional client-centred service was maintained.

Story 2 · Accountability & integrity

Taking Responsibility for a Mistake

The situation was that while I was training, a cross-check showed I'd mixed up two orders, and the dispatch records showed one parcel had already gone out with the wrong books.

My task was to take responsibility, correct the error quickly, minimise costs and communicate clearly with my manager and the customer.

I took action by informing my manager and explaining my plan to send the correct books that day. I listened to his advice about contacting the customer and asking them to hand the incorrect parcel straight back to the postman. I rang the customer, explained the mistake, reassured them and sent a prepaid return label with clear instructions.

The result was that the customer appreciated my honesty and prompt response. By anticipating their needs, communicating clearly and resolving the problem, I helped maintain their trust and strengthen our professional relationship. I also learned the importance of carefully cross-checking orders.
